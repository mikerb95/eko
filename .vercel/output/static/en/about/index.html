History · 03

A decade of milestones
in the field.

The moments that define who we are — from filing our first cases to COP16.
2013

Foundation

Ekosolv is born in Bogotá with the mission to support Colombian companies in their environmental transformation. From day one, focused on regulatory compliance and WEEE management.

2014

REP+ · ANLA

Participation in the launch of ANLA's REP+ strategy (June 14) for the first three post-consumer waste streams: lead-acid batteries, used tires, and electronic waste.

2022

Silicon Valley

Immersion in the world's most advanced sustainable technology trends. Exploration of innovations in waste management and corporate sustainability integration.

2024

COP16 · Colombia

Participation in the green zone of COP16 in Colombia. Exchange of innovative solutions with national and international environmental leaders.

2024

Quibdó · Istmina Campaign

Joint initiative with the Ministry of Environment, the National Army, Navy, Codechocó, and Universidad Antonio Nariño. Waste collection, awareness, and recycling culture in Colombia's Pacific coast.

Today

Circular economy

We lead the transition to a circular economy with our clients, installing custom containers for WEEE collection and closing the lifecycle of electronic equipment.
