The framework that
applies to your company.

A living map of the most common environmental obligations for companies that import, produce, or distribute technology in Colombia. Click to expand.

DEC 1076 · 2015

Single Environmental Sector Regulatory Decree

Consolidates most environmental regulations in Colombia. Defines licenses, permits, plans, and competent authorities.

  • MinAmbiente
  • Licenses
  • ANLA
  • CAR
RES 2184 · 2019

Color codes for recyclable waste

National standard for source separation: white (recyclable), black (non-recyclable), green (organic).

  • Separation
  • MSW
  • Households
  • Companies
RES 1297 · 2010

Post-consumer plan for Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment

Requires producers and importers to submit management, operation, coverage, and target plans.

  • WEEE
  • Post-consumer
  • Producer
  • Importer
LEY 1672 · 2013

WEEE Law

Extended producer responsibility framework. Defines principles, stakeholders, selective collection, and recovery.

  • EPR
  • Legal framework
RES 0472 · 2017

Integral management of construction and demolition waste (CDW)

Applies to companies that fit out offices, data centers, or warehouses: management plan, registration, and reporting.

  • CDW
  • Construction
RES 1407 · 2018

Environmental management plan for packaging waste

Obligation for producers and marketers of packaging placed on the Colombian market.

  • Packaging
  • EPR
  • Marketer
DEC 1076 · Title 5

Use and exploitation of water resources

Water concessions, discharge permits, retributive rates, and water use rates.

  • Water
  • Discharges
  • Concession
RES 909 · 2008

Allowable emission standards

Applies to stationary sources. Relevant for assemblers, data centers with generators, processes with furnaces.

  • Air
  • Emissions
  • Stationary sources
RES 0631 · 2015

Maximum limit parameters for point discharges

Defines what can be discharged to a water body or sewage system. Treatment plants, labs, data centers.

  • Discharges
  • BOD
  • COD
RES 1407 · 2022

Environmental penalty regime (Law 1333)

Establishes the penalty procedure and criteria for determining fines.

  • Penalties
  • Procedure
